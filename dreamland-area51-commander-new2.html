Guided training — jump straight to a track
Eight lessons per desk (nine in the tower), and the tracks are independent — start wherever you like.
New here? Take the guided training — eight short lessons that put you on live traffic from the first one. Click a strip or a radar target to select it, then issue clearances with the buttons. Type in the command line if you'd rather: land dl41, taxi jn401, tko sled21. ←→ pans the tower window, Z raises binoculars, L toggles building names. Space pauses. Separation losses and runway incursions cost you XP; clean clearances earn it, and rank buys clearance into compartments you can't see from here yet.

Talkgroups on the encrypted base system
These are talkgroups, not frequencies. The system covers the whole map — and will reach the underground areas when those open.

Green — aeronautical

1557Gnd/TwrHalf the tower job — aircraft on the ground and close in. Linked out to 123.225 VHF and 265.5 UHF for unclassified traffic. Classified airframes stay on the talkgroup and never touch the link.
1558App/DepThe other half — arrivals and departures further out. Linked to 126.150 and 261.1. Same rule: classified traffic does not go out over the link.
1559RangeDesert Radio runs the ranges from here. Flights coordinate missions and deconflict with each other. Linked to 41.00 VHF low band and 263.6 UHF. R&D range testing happens on this net.
121.5GuardDirect tower access to 121.5 and 243.0, the international distress frequencies. A classified aircraft in trouble would not use Guard — it would call on 1557, 1558 or 1559.

Tan — base operations

2001Command PostBase announcements and anything that crosses disciplines. The automated weather goes out here every three hours. Security asking Fire for a unit, Security asking for barricades, Fire asking for a clean-up, R&D Safety asking for apparatus — all of it is requested here and then worked on the owning desk.
2002Base OpsThe maintenance desk. Electrical, utilities, water, grounds, facilities and the crews that hold them up.

Orange — fire and medical

2005EMSMedical side of the fire desk. The desk dispatches medics here, and the base hospital monitors it for inbound patients, gives receiving instructions and posts its own bed status.
2006FireAircraft rescue and firefighting, and structural work on the installation.
2007WildfireWildland operations, including the airtanker. Linked to 170.025, the Nevada wildfire coordination frequency.
2008Nye CoMutual aid with Nye County Fire and the town departments. Linked to 155.625, the county repeater. It also lets the fire desk hear what the county is dealing with.

Blue — security

2201SF GateGate and perimeter — the outer watch.
2202SF BaseInterior security — the inner watch.
2203SF TacSpecial security incidents, and the working net for the Office of Special Investigations.
2204SheriffLinked to the Nye County Sheriff repeater on 155.430. Mostly listening, so the base knows what the county is doing — and so OSI hears it when a citizen reports something they should not have seen.
2205NHPLinked to the Highway Patrol talkgroup on the state 800 MHz system. Same idea: mostly listening, occasionally the first warning that something came down where the public can reach it.
